I read a thing by McLuhan about fire being a kind of dialogue in itself. A scene or chapter for one of my scripts could explore that idea, and literally have it as part of the scene.

The point is embedding meaning, the way filmmaking or writing can have many layers. There are two ways to put people by a fire:

1. They talk by a fire because that is a setting you could choose, sort of choose-your-own-adventure storytelling.
2. The scene deliberately explores a theme, and the theme, the setting, the dialogue and the meaning are all purposeful.

The second is the one I want.
