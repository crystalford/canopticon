Whey protein that dissolves in hot black coffee

The problem

A scoop of whey — 20 to 30 grams — that disappears into hot black coffee the way collagen does: no clumps, no curdling, no taste. It does not exist, and the reason is chemistry, not powder.

Drafted with Claude from the grounding · awaiting approval

1Why it is unsolved

You cannot buy a whey protein today that goes into hot black coffee at 20–30 g the way collagen does: instantly, with no taste and no texture. opening
Coffee's pH (about 4.85–5.13) sits right at whey's isoelectric point opening
The industry is working around hot black coffee, not solving it. Key finding 5

2Who is closest

It is the closest ingredient conceptually to what you want (heat- and acid-stabilized pure BLG), but it is aimed at UHT drinks and fresh dairy, and there is no published hot-coffee data. Section 3 · Verley MicroStab

3When, and whether

My rough estimate for a purpose-built "hot black coffee whey" launch is 2027–2029. No company has announced one. Verdict
A fully neutral-tasting, texture-free 25–30 g whey dose in 85 °C black coffee may never fully happen Verdict

4Meanwhile

Let coffee drop to roughly 55–60 °C (drinkable-warm) before adding intact whey. Practical tips
