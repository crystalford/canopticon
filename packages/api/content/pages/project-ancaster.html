Ancaster

Eighteen research passes toward a publisher-owned layer that lets a reader keep a recommendation and come back to it without the recommender losing credit. The passes cover how long real purchases are delayed, which attribution rules survive that delay, what the platforms permit, and the experiment that would prove the thesis or kill it.

One expression matches a series marker — “Pass N”, “Pass Six”, “Nth Pass”, “Research Pass N”, “A Mechanistic Research Pass”, with or without the project's name, at the front, after a dash or colon at the end, or in parentheses — and removes it from the headline and the slug. All sixteen real titles in the archive matched; the two that carry a marker but no project keep order without joining one. The project's own name goes only when it is the prefix that filed the document. Nothing else in the title is touched; if the expression does not match, nothing changes. The number is kept as order, so the chain still reads top to bottom; two documents with the same number, or a gap, simply sort by date. The original title is kept verbatim, shown in the rail as “titled by the author”, emitted as Also titled, and never altered in the .md twin, because the body is never edited.
