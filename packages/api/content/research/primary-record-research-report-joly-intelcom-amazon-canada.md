# Primary-Record Research Report: Joly–Intelcom–Amazon Canada

**Scope:** Targeted retrieval of (1) Office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner public-registry filings, (2) federal Registry of Lobbyists filings, and (3) federal Open Government grants and contributions disclosures, in connection with Minister Mélanie Joly, her brother Jean-Sébastien Joly, Intelcom Courrier Canada Inc. / Dragonfly Shipping, and Amazon Canada.

**Standard applied:** Zero editorializing. Only findings present in the primary record (or, where the primary record could not be directly retrieved, explicit acknowledgment of that limitation) are reported below.

---

## QUERY 1 — Ethics Commissioner Public Registry: Conflict-of-Interest Screen

### Primary record located

A single conflict-of-interest screen connecting Mélanie Joly to Intelcom Courrier Canada Inc. is published on the public registry of the Office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner of Canada:

- **Declaration No. 36909**
- **URL of attached PDF:** `https://prciec-rpccie.parl.gc.ca/Lists/Declarations/Attachments/36909/Public%20declaration%20voluntary%20screen_Intelcom_October%202022.pdf`
- **Date of public declaration:** October 2022
- **Administered by:** the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs and Minister Joly's Chief of Staff
- **Parties informed in writing (per the document):** the Prime Minister, the Clerk of the Privy Council and Secretary to Cabinet, the Parliamentary Secretaries to the Minister of Foreign Affairs, and members of the Minister's ministerial staff
- **Legal basis cited in the document:** section 29 of the *Conflict of Interest Act*, with reference to the recusal duty under section 21
- **Operative text (verbatim from the document):** the screen is "an appropriate measure to prevent any opportunity while exercising my official powers, duties and functions to further the private interests of my common-law partner, Mr. Jean-Sébastien Joly, President and CEO as well as owner of Intelcom Courrier Canada Inc., by giving preferential treatment to his company that may have future dealings with the Government of Canada." The screen is to ensure "I am neither made aware of nor a participant in any discussions or decision-making processes pertaining to the private interests of my brother and/or his company, unless the interest in the decision or matter is one of general application."

### Anomaly in the primary record

The October 2022 declaration contains an apparent internal inconsistency: the first operative paragraph describes Jean-Sébastien Joly as Mélanie Joly's "common-law partner," while the subsequent paragraph (and all corroborating public-record reporting) describes him as her "brother." This anomaly is present in the document published on the Commissioner's registry and is reproduced here verbatim because the research standard is zero editorializing.

### Updated/new screen following the May 2025 portfolio change to Industry

- **Finding:** Searches of the public registry and of the open web (including site-restricted searches against `prciec-rpccie.parl.gc.ca`) returned **no new or updated voluntary conflict-of-interest screen** filed by Minister Joly in connection with Intelcom or Jean-Sébastien Joly following her swearing-in on **13 May 2025** as Minister of Industry and Minister responsible for Canada Economic Development for Quebec Regions.
- **Significance per the primary record:** the only published screen on the public registry remains the October 2022 instrument, whose administering officials are named as the **Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs** and the Chief of Staff. Following the May 2025 portfolio move, the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs no longer has hierarchical or operational oversight of Joly's ministerial activities (which are now at Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) and at the Economic Development Agency of Canada for the Regions of Quebec (CED-Q)). As of the date of this search, no replacement screen designating ISED's Deputy Minister or the CED-Q Deputy Minister/President has been published on the public registry.
- **Public statements by the Ethics Commissioner's office post-May 2025:** No public statement specific to the Joly-Intelcom situation issued by the Commissioner's office after 13 May 2025 was located in this research. The only Commissioner's-office statement on Joly-Intelcom-Amazon located in any record is a comment reported by *Journal des voisins* during the spring 2025 federal election in response to a complaint letter from Conservative candidates Pierre Paul-Hus and Michael Barrett — the Director of Communications of the Commissariat aux conflits d'intérêts et à l'éthique stated: "Il n'y a actuellement aucune enquête en cours en vertu de la Loi" ("There is no investigation currently underway under the Act"), and noted that once Parliament is dissolved MPs lose standing to request investigations. The Commissioner's office did not, in that statement, comment on the substance of a new or updated screen.
- **Statements by the Minister's office:** Press Secretary Catherine Mounier-Desrochers stated (publicly reported during the same April 2025 complaint correspondence) that Minister Joly "s'est toujours récusée proactivement de toute affaire concernant Amazon ou Intelcom, que cela soit dans le contexte du cabinet ou dans le cadre de ses fonctions ministérielles." This is a statement by the Minister's office, not a primary record from the Ethics Commissioner.

### Access limitations (Query 1)

- The Commissioner's public registry (`prciec-rpccie.parl.gc.ca`) does not expose stable, search-engine-indexed declaration listings for systematic third-party search; declaration pages are addressed by GUID. One additional Joly declaration URL was identified in search results (`DeclarationID=42665cc3-6a55-ed11-8167-001dd8b72449`), but it could not be fetched in this session.
- An exhaustive enumeration of every Joly declaration on the registry would require browsing the registry interactively (or filing for an index via the Commissioner's office). This was not possible within the research scope here; the conclusion of "no new screen" is based on the absence of any such document appearing in (a) targeted searches of the registry domain, (b) general web search, and (c) the cited journalistic and advocacy coverage from March–November 2025, all of which continue to describe the October 2022 screen as the only one in force.

## QUERY 2 — Federal Registry of Lobbyists

### Access limitation (Query 2) — critical

The Office of the Commissioner of Lobbying's Registry (`lobbycanada.gc.ca`) blocks direct fetching of dynamic search-results pages by automated retrieval (CSRF-protected forms; the search-result URLs are session-keyed and were not returnable in this session). The primary-record search functions accessible to a human user at `https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/advSrch` could not be exercised programmatically here. The findings below are therefore based on (a) snippet evidence from indexed Registry pages and (b) corroborating reporting that cited the Registry directly.

### Intelcom Courrier Canada Inc. / Dragonfly Shipping registrations

- **Direct registration evidence located:** No in-house corporation registration, no consultant registration, and no monthly communication report with **Intelcom Courrier Canada Inc.**, **Intelcom Express**, **Intelcom Group**, or **Dragonfly Shipping** as the named client, organization, or corporation surfaced in any search returning Registry pages. Search snippets returning the keyword "Intelcom" on `lobbycanada.gc.ca` produced only results for the unrelated companies **Intel of Canada, Ltd.** (registration 948970-373756) and **Intel Technology of Canada, ULC** (registration 948970-370021) — i.e., the semiconductor manufacturer, not the courier. No registration filed under the name Intelcom (the courier) or Dragonfly Shipping was identified.
- **Provisional conclusion (subject to direct registry verification):** the public-record evidence developed in this research is consistent with Intelcom **not having any active or historical federal lobbying registration in its own corporate name**. This is consistent with the Lobbying Act threshold — registration is required only where lobbying constitutes a "significant part" of an employee's duties — but is a notable finding given the company's federal customer base (it was 50% owned by Canada Post until 2007) and its receipt of BDC equity investment. The investigator is advised to verify directly via the Registry's interactive interface before publication.

### Amazon's lobbying communications with Mélanie Joly's offices

- **Aggregate figure (sourced to Radio-Canada via the federal Registry, 2017):** Between October 2016 and October 2017, Amazon recorded **99 registered communications** with the Trudeau government. CBC News (3 November 2017) confirmed the figure and noted, citing the Registry, that then-Heritage Minister Mélanie Joly's office had multiple meetings with major U.S. tech firms — including five registered Google meetings with Minister Joly and six with her then-Chief of Staff Leslie Church (a former Google Canada director of communications and public affairs). CBC's article focused on Google's contacts with Minister Joly specifically; for Amazon's 99 communications, the CBC article did not name-disaggregate by minister. (Source URL: `https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/amazon-google-lobby-trudeau-1.4379761`.)
- **The Radio-Canada source article** (cited by *L'aut'journal*, 23 May 2025): "Google, Amazon, Microsoft et Facebook à l'assaut du gouvernement canadien," Radio-Canada, 31 October 2017, URL `https://ici.radio-canada.ca/nouvelle/1064360/...`. That story reports that within the 99 Amazon communications, contacts were registered with the office of Mélanie Joly during her tenure as Minister of Canadian Heritage. The author of the *L'aut'journal* piece (Frédéric Lacroix-Couture) draws on the Registry via this Radio-Canada compilation.
- **12-Month Lobbying Activity Search snippet (Registry, retrieved May 2026):** A Registry search filtered by `dpohFullName=HONOURABLE MELANIE JOLY` returns active lobbying activities targeting Minister Joly; subject-matter categories listed in the result-set headers include: Environment, Transportation, Climate, Economic Development, Health, Immigration, Industry, Infrastructure, International Relations, International Trade, Labour, Regional Development, Taxation and Finance, Tourism, Pensions, Security, Arts and Culture, Research and Development, Official Languages, Housing, Municipalities, and Consumer Issues. The result page identified two active registrations (1 Consultant, 1 In-house Corporation) currently targeting Joly. The detail-level identity of those registrants and a count of monthly communication reports were not retrievable in this session (search-results page blocked by `robots.txt` / dynamic-session restriction).
- **One specific February 2026 communication with Minister Joly identified in indexed Registry snippets:** Communication No. 5695-671255, posted 2026-03-05, communication date 2026-02-26, naming "**Mélanie Joly, Minister of Industry and Minister responsible for Canada Economic Development for Quebec Regions** | Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED)" and "**Yasmin Atassi, Senior Policy Advisor** | Minister of Industry…" The subject-matter categories listed are: Defence, Employment and Training, Energy, Government Procurement, Industry, Infrastructure, International Relations, International Trade, Science and Technology, Taxation and Finance, Transportation, Research and Development, Privacy and Access to Information, Economic Development, Foreign Affairs. The identity of the registrant for communication 5695-671255 was not visible in the snippet retrieved. (URL: `https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/rcntSmCmLgs?dt=2026-02&cid=43&tb=historical`.)
- **A July 2025 monthly communication** also surfaced indirectly, naming "Mélanie Joly, Minister of Foreign Affairs | Global Affairs Canada (GAC)" as a DPOH (Communication No. 270204-642856, dated 2025-07-09, posted 2025-08-15) — note that the date of communication post-dates her 13 May 2025 transfer to Industry, but the registrant filed the DPOH-position field as "Minister of Foreign Affairs." This may be a filing-error or a reference to a meeting prior to the portfolio change; the underlying registrant identity was not in the snippet retrieved. (URL: `https://lobbycanada.gc.ca/app/secure/ocl/lrs/do/rcntGiCmLgs?rt=1&dt=2025-07&cid=212&tb=historical&pg=3`.)
- **Amazon-specific communications with Joly's offices post-May 2025:** None were specifically identifiable in the snippet evidence retrieved. The Registry contains entries naming "Amazon Web Services Canada, Inc." in the same period (a snippet showed AWS Canada associated with a "Caroline Séguin, Director of Outreach and Strategic Initiatives, Office of the Minister of Foreign Affairs" entry — that DPOH worked in Joly's GAC ministerial office during her tenure there). Granular date and subject information for any Amazon Canada or AWS Canada meeting specifically with Minister Joly cannot be reported here without direct interactive Registry verification.

### Subjects searched for in lobbying communications and not located in the retrieved snippets

No lobbying communication explicitly cross-referencing Intelcom, Canada Post privatization, parcel-delivery regulation, gig-worker classification, or last-mile-courier labour policy was located via the indexed snippet evidence available in this session. **Absence in the snippet evidence is not equivalent to absence in the Registry**; this is an access limitation and a journalist verifying for publication should run the Advanced Registry Search interactively (Document type: Registrations and Monthly Communication Reports; Client/organization/corporation name: "Intelcom"; and again "Dragonfly"; and again sweep all monthly communications with DPOH "Joly" filtered by subject category "Labour," "Transportation," and "Industry" since 2015).

## QUERY 3 — Federal Open Government Grants and Contributions Database

### Access limitation (Query 3) — critical

The federal Open Government grants and contributions search interface (`search.open.canada.ca/grants/`) blocks programmatic retrieval (`robots.txt` denies the search endpoint). Direct execution of keyword searches against the dataset at `open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/432527ab-7922-401d-a329-05b33f1de4c8` could not be performed in this session. Findings below are derived from open-web indexing of records published on Government of Canada properties.

### Federal financial assistance to Intelcom / Dragonfly / Jean-Sébastien Joly — findings

- **Grants and contributions database (CED-Q, ISED, regional development agencies):** No grant, contribution, transfer payment, or other recorded transfer-payment award to **Intelcom Courrier Canada Inc.**, **Intelcom Express**, **Intelcom Group**, **Dragonfly Shipping**, or **Jean-Sébastien Joly** was located in indexed Government of Canada open-data results from any search executed in this research. The CED-Q grants and contributions index for the period in question (the canonical URL `https://search.open.canada.ca/grants/?owner_org=ced-dec`) returned no Intelcom or Dragonfly record in any indexed snippet.
- **Confirmation note (subject to direct verification):** The investigator should re-execute the search directly via the open-data interface with the following search strings: "Intelcom", "Intelcom Courrier", "Intelcom Express", "Dragonfly", "Dragonfly Shipping", "Joly, Jean-Sébastien", at both `search.open.canada.ca/grants` and `search.open.canada.ca/contracts` (proactive disclosure of contracts is a separate dataset). The proactive disclosure of contracts dataset (over-$10,000 federal contracts) is distinct from the grants and contributions dataset queried here.

### Business Development Bank of Canada (BDC) — equity rather than grant

- **Primary record located:** BDC Capital's investment portfolio page lists **Intelcom Courrier** as a portfolio company.
- **URL:** `https://www.bdc.ca/fr/bdc-capital/investissement-croissance/portefeuille/intelcom-courrier-canada`
- **BDC portfolio description (verbatim, French):** "Intelcom Courrier Canada Inc. Intelcom est une entreprise de livraison de colis en 24 ou 48 heures qui offre des services de messagerie et de distribution ainsi que d'autres services spécialisés."
- **Form of federal financial involvement:** Equity investment by **BDC Capital** (BDC's investment arm) participating in the **2017 management buy-out** by which Jean-Sébastien Joly acquired Intelcom from founder Daniel Hudon, jointly with the Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec (CDPQ). This is corroborated by multiple secondary sources (Globe and Mail, 6 April 2021; La Presse, 11 October 2023; La Presse, 15 July 2025), which describe the participation of "la Caisse de dépôt et de la Banque de développement du Canada (BDC)" in the 2017 buy-out. Mr. Joly stated in his July 2025 La Presse interview: "On a racheté la participation de la BDC et la Caisse détient moins de 20 % de nos actions." This indicates BDC's equity stake has since been redeemed.
- **Exact amount, date, and program of the BDC equity participation:** **Not disclosed on the BDC public portfolio page.** BDC Capital does not publish the dollar amount, transaction date, or specific program/fund of its portfolio-company investments on the public-facing portfolio page; these are not transfer payments and accordingly do not appear in the federal grants and contributions disclosure database. They would generally be available only via Access to Information request to BDC, which is itself subject to confidentiality protections under the *Business Development Bank of Canada Act*. **This is an ATIP-required disclosure for any forensic dollar-precise reporting.**
- **Significance for the research record:** federal financial support to Intelcom is documented in primary records as **a BDC Capital equity investment**, not as a grant or contribution. Federal grants-and-contributions search results returned no Intelcom record in this research.

### Canada Post historical ownership stake (context, not within the grant database scope)

- Canada Post Corporation, a federal Crown corporation, purchased a **50% equity stake** in Intelcom Express in 2000; that stake was repurchased by Intelcom in 2007. Source: Wikipedia (Intelcom), corroborated by *Canadian Dimension* (2025) reporting that the Canada Post purchase was authorized under then-CPC CEO André Ouellet. This is not a transfer payment and does not appear in the grants and contributions database; it is recorded for context only.

### Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec (CDPQ) — not federal

The CDPQ is a Quebec provincial pension-fund manager, not a federal funder. Its investment in Intelcom (jointly with BDC in 2017, and still <20% as of mid-2025 per the CEO's own statement) is outside the scope of the federal grants and contributions database. Recorded here only to disambiguate the source of capital.
