# Ancaster Pass 10: Traffic Permissions, Paid Amplification, Signup Attribution, Earnings Aggregation, In-House Program and Account Model

Chris's belief that he can't plug into PartnerStack is wrong. PartnerStack has a documented partner-side API (Actions, Customers, Partnerships, Payouts, Rewards, Transactions, authenticated with a partner-generated Bearer key) and partner postbacks, and Strackr already sells PartnerStack earnings aggregation built on exactly that key.\[1\]\[2\] So Ancaster can show third-party earnings and customers for PartnerStack creators with consent. Ancaster still can't show the merchant's internal funnel beyond what the network exposes. Separately, Ancaster must treat "boosting your own post" as paid social, and for ElevenLabs that is prohibited outright.\[3\]

## TL;DR

- **Traffic:** Encode permissions per program across 12 traffic classes, with four states: allowed, prohibited, needs approval, not stated. Treat a boost of your own post containing an affiliate link as paid social. For ElevenLabs it is prohibited on three grounds: marks in any paid-ad component, targeting marks, and "any paid placement to direct users, whether directly or indirectly."\[3\] beehiiv is the only program in this sweep that explicitly allows paid social (Meta, LinkedIn, X) while banning search ads, and it also ships a real link-embedded consumer offer.\[4\]\[5\]
- **Attribution and data:** PartnerStack-style programs attribute at the customer level. If the free signup happens inside the cookie window (90 days for ElevenLabs), later paid upgrades credit that partner.\[6\] ElevenLabs pays 22% for the first 12 months of a non-free-tier subscription,\[7\] so "Start free" should be the primary CTA, as long as the signup runs through the creator's link. For third-party programs, build API-key/postback aggregation (PartnerStack first, then Dub and Impact). For in-house offers, build a first-party click ID with Stripe-webhook ledger and an outbound postback/webhook API.
- **Amplification and accounts:** Don't buy media or hold spend. v1 should be a "boost kit": permission checks, disclosure-correct creative, UTM/click-ID links to the creator's own page, and deep links into each platform's native boost/partnership flow. Hold off on direct ad-API writes until one platform approves. Google's Explorer access needs only an "Apply for access" click in Cloud Console and is sometimes granted automatically; LinkedIn's Development tier can edit up to 5 ad accounts. Use one account with capability-based workspaces (publish / advertise / manage clients) and intent-based onboarding, not split publisher/advertiser signups.

## Key Findings

1. **PartnerStack partner data is accessible.** PartnerStack's help center says the Partner API "allows you to return a list of Actions, Customers, Marketplace listings, Partnerships, Payouts (Withdrawals), Rewards (Commissions), or Transactions" and that it "uses API key to authenticate requests… via Bearer authentication." The article is explicitly written for partners who want "to access your partner data using external services."\[1\] Strackr's PartnerStack connector tells users to "Create a new key for Strackr and paste this API key on Strackr." Its PartnerStack docs page lists "Network ≈ 700 affiliate programs" (Strackr's own approximation). One limit: "If you are a member of a partner team, you will need to use the API Key generated by the partner team owner."\[1\]
2. **ElevenLabs' free-signup rule.** A "Referred Paying Subscriber" is "someone who clicks through a Participant's Affiliate Link, subsequently registers for and maintains a non-free-tier subscription… and is properly attributed to such Participant through our tracking system." Commission is 22% (Starter/Creator/Pro/Scale) or 11% (Business) on purchases "during the first 12 months of such Referred Paying Subscribers' subscription"; enterprise is excluded\[7\]\[8\] (Program Terms, last updated 10 June 2026).\[3\] ElevenLabs' partner guide defines dashboard "Signups" as people "(both paid and unpaid) who signed up… within 90 days of clicking your link."\[7\] PartnerStack's attribution doc says that if a customer "signs up or completes a purchase within the 90-day window… all subsequent activities and actions will be attributed to that partner."\[6\] **Inference (not stated verbatim by ElevenLabs):** a free signup through the link within 90 days that upgrades later earns commission. The 12-month clock appears to start at the paid subscription, not at signup.
3. **Boosting counts as paid placement under strict terms.** ElevenLabs' Paid Media clause bans (a) targeting its marks "on a paid search, paid social, app store, or other paid advertising platform," (b) including its marks "in any component of a paid advertisement," and (c) "any paid placement to direct users, whether directly or indirectly, to any ElevenLabs website or property."\[3\] A boosted post is a paid placement. If it carries the link, it directs users at least indirectly, and naming ElevenLabs in it trips (b). Most other terms are silent on "boosting" and only address "PPC" or "search engine ads."
4. **Few dev/AI programs offer a real consumer deal.** In the sweep, only beehiiv (link-embedded "14-day free trial and 20% off of their first 3 months") and Runway (code: "Your followers get 25% off") had affiliate-facing consumer offers.\[4\]\[9\] Descript ("discount codes are not available to affiliates") and Kinsta ("we don't provide exclusive discounts to specific affiliate partners") explicitly refuse.\[10\]\[11\]
5. **Several beachhead brands have no open cash program.** Canva (Canvassador, "currently closed"), Cursor (none found), Bolt.new ("doesn't currently have affiliate or sponsorship programs"), Replit (credit-only referral), Vercel, Supabase (none found), Notion ("currently not accepting new affiliates") and Zapier (partners-only) are not viable v1 third-party offers.\[12\]\[13\]\[14\]\[15\]\[16\]
6. **Ad API access is gated but possible for a solo developer on some platforms.** Google's Access Levels doc says Explorer access lets a Cloud project "execute up to 2,880 operations per day against production accounts." You request it with an "Apply for access" button; PPC Land reports automatic grants only "in certain cases." Explorer also blocks billing, account creation and Keyword Planner. LinkedIn's Development tier allows edits on up to 5 ad accounts.\[17\] Meta needs Business Verification plus ads_management Advanced Access before other businesses' accounts can authorize an app.\[18\] X, TikTok and Reddit require separate approvals or allow-listing.\[19\]\[20\]\[21\]

## 1. Traffic Permissions, Precisely

### Taxonomy (the schema Ancaster should store per program)

Each program gets a `traffic_policy` with one state per class: `allowed | prohibited | needs_approval | not_stated`. Each state carries a `conditions[]` array (e.g., `negative_brand_keywords_required`, `must_land_on_own_page`, `no_marks_in_ad_creative`, `platform_policy_compliance`) plus `source_url`, `source_quote` and `verified_at`.

| # | Class | Definition (what Ancaster checks) |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Organic social | Unpaid post, video, bio link or description on a creator-owned channel |
| T2 | Self-boost of organic post | Creator pays the platform to amplify their *own* existing post (X Promote, Meta Boost, TikTok Promote, LinkedIn boost) |
| T3 | Paid social from creator's ad account | New ad campaign run by the creator (Ads Manager, X Ads, Reddit Ads) |
| T4 | Partnership/Spark/whitelisted ads run by advertiser | The *merchant* pays, using the creator's identity (Spark code, Meta partnership ad code, YouTube partnership ads, LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads) |
| T5 | Paid search, non-brand | Bidding on generic terms ("AI voice generator") |
| T6 | Paid search, brand/trademark | Bidding on or including the merchant's marks, misspellings, "+coupon" variants |
| T7 | Native/display | Taboola/Outbrain, programmatic display, pop-under |
| T8 | Email/newsletter | Sends to the creator's own opted-in list |
| T9 | Direct linking from ads | Ad URL is the affiliate link or merchant site (vs T9b: ad lands on creator's own page) |
| T10 | Coupon/deal sites | Publishing codes on sites whose primary purpose is coupons |
| T11 | Incentivized/cashback | Paying or rewarding the user for clicking or buying |
| T12 | Sub-affiliates | Reselling or delegating links to other promoters or networks |

**Key rule: T2 is paid social.** A self-boost uses the ad platform's auction and billing. Under any clause banning "paid social," "paid placement," or "sponsored advertisements on social media platforms," it is covered. Where terms only ban "PPC"/"search engine ads," T2 is `not_stated` and should default to `needs_approval`, with a one-click "ask the program manager" template.

**Ad-platform destination policy.** Google's "Insufficient original content" policy lists "Bridge pages, doorways, gateways, or other intermediate pages that are only used to link to other sites" as violations. It also advises "useful, unique, and original content."\[55\] Ancaster's interstitial (endorsement → try-it moment → code → CTA) is structurally a bridge page. It passes only if the page carries real original value: the creator's own demo, comparison or instructions. Meta, TikTok, X and Reddit affiliate-landing-page policies were not re-verified in this pass. Treat them as "must comply with platform policy" and don't claim compliance.

**What Ancaster can offer without buying media or holding spend:**
1. A permission gate: before any boost, check `traffic_policy.T2/T3`, the brand-keyword and marks-in-creative conditions, and the landing destination (T9b only).
2. A boost kit: pre-written, disclosure-correct copy; the creator's page URL with a boost-specific click ID/UTM (so paid traffic is measured separately); and deep links into the native X/Meta/TikTok boost flow.
3. A T4 broker: Ancaster already has a permission ledger for Spark/partnership codes, so in-house advertisers (Chris's products) can run creator content as their own ads, with spend on the advertiser's card.
4. Later, direct API writes on Google (Explorer) and LinkedIn (Development tier), the two platforms a solo developer can realistically be approved on.

## 3. Signup Attribution and Free Tiers

- **Customer-level vs purchase-time cookie.** PartnerStack creates a customer at signup and ties all later transactions to that customer's partner: "you must create a customer to represent a referred sign up BEFORE triggering any commissions."\[56\] Payment webhooks (Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly, Chargify) then match on customer_key/provider_key.\[57\]\[58\] Cookie failures (browser switch, cleared cache) lose the referral unless the vendor manually re-attributes it.\[59\] The cookie window therefore matters at signup, not at purchase.
- **Dub, Impact, Rewardful, FirstPromoter, Tolt:** their lead→sale models were not re-verified in this pass. Dub's partner postbacks (lead.created, sale.created, commission.created) indicate the same lead-then-sale customer model.
- **Programs paying for free signups:** Notion ("up to $50 per activated sign up," closed to new affiliates)\[12\] and Grammarly (qualified action "An Account Sign-Up and a Purchase," pairing a sign-up with a purchase).\[41\] No other program in the sweep pays CPL for free signups.
- **ElevenLabs:** see Key Finding 2. Free signup via link within 90 days → later paid upgrade → 22% for the first 12 months of paid subscription, payable after a 90-day hold.\[3\]\[7\] The terms don't state a maximum gap between signup and upgrade. Chris should confirm with his ElevenLabs partner manager via PartnerStack and record the answer in the Endorsement Ledger.
- **Freemium conversion evidence:** ChartMogul/Growth Unhinged/ProductLed's "The Conversion Report" (Kyle Poyar, 4 Feb 2026; 200 B2B products, conversion within 6 months) reports "The median free-to-paid conversion rate across all products is 8%," and credit-card-required trials converting at 30%.\[60\] ChartMogul is a vendor, but the method is disclosed. For freemium specifically, the same report says "On average, 3%-5% is a GOOD conversion rate for a freemium self-serve product, and 8%-12% is GREAT." So a creator's 100 free signups plausibly yield single-digit paid customers. Ancaster's value is making sure those 100 signups are attributed.

**Page design implication:** For free-tier programs, the primary CTA should be "Start free with [creator]'s link." The code, if any, should be shown as "use at upgrade," and a secondary line should say "paid plans: [offer]." The single disclosed affiliate CTA stays the only exit. Send a post-signup nurture through the Publisher Funnel with the same tracked link, because a second click through the same partner link refreshes that partner's cookie on a new device.

## 4. Real Consumer Offers

- **How offers are issued:** link-embedded (beehiiv: trial plus discount applied through the partner link), creator code (Runway 25% off; Descript's separate Cello referral gives 50% off Creator for 2 months), network promo codes (Impact/Awin; Hostinger forbids using other affiliates' codes), or none (Descript affiliate, Kinsta).\[4\]\[9\]\[10\]\[34\]\[61\]
- **Frequency:** In this sweep, 2 of the roughly 13 programs with readable terms offered an affiliate-facing consumer deal. Unique per-creator codes are the exception in dev/AI SaaS.
- **Database recommendation:** separate `program` (commission, cookie, network, traffic_policy) from `offer` (type: discount_pct | extended_trial | bonus_credits | free_tier_only; mechanism: link | shared_code | unique_code; eligibility; expiry; source_quote; verified_at; verification_method). A program with no `offer` rows is shown as "no deal — free tier" or "no deal." Only offers with a verified row get the "actual offer" badge.
- **Keeping offers current:** weekly re-fetch of source pages with diff alerts; creator-reported "code works" checks; and, for Dub/PartnerStack programs, offer fields read via API where exposed. Expire badges at 30 days without re-verification.

## 6. The In-House "Ancaster Official Program"

**Tracking architecture (Cloudflare-native):**
1. Click: a Worker at the creator's sayable link mints `anc_click_id` (ULID), writes the click to D1 via Queue, sets a first-party cookie on the landing page and appends `?anc=` to the product's signup URL.
2. Signup: Chris's product stores `anc_click_id` and `partner_id` on the Stripe customer (metadata) at account creation. This is customer-level lock-in, with a configurable window (default 90 days, matching industry norms).
3. Coupon attribution: each partner gets a Stripe promotion code. A redemption with no click ID attributes by code, and code beats cookie when they conflict (the code-first reconciliation already specified).
4. Conversion: Stripe webhooks (`customer.created`, `invoice.paid`, `charge.refunded`, `customer.subscription.updated/deleted`) → Queue → ledger in D1. Paddle/Lemon Squeezy use equivalent webhooks. Each ledger event emits a signed Referral Receipt.
5. **Outbound Partner/Postback API:** (a) HMAC-signed JSON webhooks per event (`click`, `signup`, `trial_started`, `sale`, `renewal`, `refund`, `commission.approved`); (b) templated S2S GET postbacks so any tracker (Everflow, Voluum, RedTrack, Binom) can receive conversions. Ancaster substitutes macros such as `{click_id}`, `{sub1..sub5}`, `{event}`, `{payout}`, `{revenue}`, `{currency}`, `{txn_id}` into the partner's URL. Accept an inbound `sub_id`/external click ID on the link so the partner's tracker ID round-trips. (Macro names follow common tracker conventions; exact per-tracker syntax was not verified in this pass.)
6. Publisher analytics: clicks, unique visitors, signups, trials, paid conversions, MRR attributed, refunds, commission states, and EPC by link/code/placement (including boost-tagged traffic).

**Fraud controls:** block self-referral (partner email/domain or Stripe card fingerprint matches the buyer); hold commissions 30–60 days past first payment (ElevenLabs uses 90 days for chargeback risk);\[7\] velocity and ASN/datacenter-IP checks on clicks; disposable-email and duplicate-device checks on free signups; code-leak detection (redemptions from coupon-site referrers).

**Payout precedents:** Dub pays partners through Stripe Express (PayPal fallback), and "Commissions are payable only after the applicable Client has paid Dub in full." Its terms also require that commissions from its clients "must be processed through Dub," a platform-collected, custody model.\[67\] Dub's own comparison says Rewardful doesn't run payouts and has merchants mark payouts paid; that is a competitor's claim.\[68\] Dub's partner payout thresholds differ between its help page ($10 auto-deposit) and blog ($100 default, with $2 fee for lower custom amounts), which suggests configurable thresholds. International bank payouts carry a 1% FX fee.\[68\]\[69\]

**Adapting "no custody":** For Chris's *own* products, paying his own affiliates from his own business account isn't custody of anyone else's money, so the principle survives intact. For *outside advertisers*, collecting their money to pay partners would make Ancaster a funds intermediary. That raises money-services and KYC questions in Canada and the US that were not researched in this pass and need a lawyer. Tax forms (W-9/W-8BEN for US-linked payees, 1099-NEC for US persons, Canadian T4A/slip obligations) also were not verified here. Get an accountant's answer before paying non-Canadian affiliates at scale.

## 7. One Account vs Roles

**Precedents (from documentation read):** PartnerStack separates Partner API (Bearer key) from Vendor API (Basic auth),\[70\] and Dub runs a distinct partner portal and Partner Terms from its workspace product.\[67\] Both split roles at the data and contract layer. The unified-product precedents (beehiiv Boosts, SparkLoop, Passionfroot, Stripe, Shopify Collabs, Linktree) were not re-verified in this pass. They're cited as general design patterns, not sourced facts.

**Trade-offs:**
- **Unified:** one signup, a creator can also be an advertiser (Chris himself), agencies can hold many workspaces.
- **Split:** cleaner trust boundaries, separate controller/processor roles (the advertiser is controller of buyer data; Ancaster is processor), and simpler fraud rules.

**Recommendation:** one identity, many workspaces. Each workspace has capabilities (`publish`, `advertise`, `manage_clients`) switched on by an intent question at onboarding ("Promote products / Sell my product / Manage clients"). Keep separate ledgers per capability. Hard-block self-referral across capabilities: a workspace can't earn commission on its own advertised offer from its own users. Agencies get membership in client workspaces with scoped roles.

- Fewer than 3 of 5 pilot creators connect a PartnerStack key → drop native aggregation and use CSV.

## Caveats

- Several program cells rely on secondary sources (Semrush, Lovable restrictions, Hostinger SEM list, Riverside cookie). They're marked in the table and need re-verification before being shown to creators as fact.
- The ElevenLabs free-signup→upgrade rule is inferred from its terms, partner guide and PartnerStack docs, not stated as one sentence. The guide's FAQ "Can I run Google Ads with this affiliate link?" answer was not visible in the fetched text.\[7\]
- Meta's rate-tier threshold is 500 calls in 15 days, lowered from 1,500 per Meta's developer blog; secondary sources date the change to 4 May 2026. Dub's payout threshold also differs between its help page and blog.\[68\]\[69\]
- Not researched in this pass: Microsoft Advertising API, ChatGPT Ads, Rewardful/FirstPromoter/Tolt payout mechanics, CJ/Awin/Rakuten APIs, tax-form and money-transmission law, and per-platform affiliate ad policies other than Google's.
