MACHLOKET · MARKED MAR 14 MACHLOKET
Whether Canadian housing financialization is primarily a supply-side regulatory problem or primarily a monetary-architecture artifact.
POSITION A · ORTHODOX
Insufficient construction permits, restrictive zoning, and slow approval processes are the binding constraints; supply expansion is the corrective.
CMHC research, multiple A-grade sources
POSITION B · HETERODOX
Credit-creation patterns, mortgage-securitization design, and capital-gains tax treatment make housing a financial asset class regardless of supply; structural reform is the corrective.
Lavoie/Seccareccia, Gabor, multiple B-grade sources
Why machloket: both positions have substantial empirical support. Supply analyses correctly identify binding constraints in specific markets; financialization analyses correctly identify constraints invariant to supply changes in other markets. The synthesis would require evidence neither tradition has assembled. Holding both as live preserves analytical completeness; collapsing would force a premature methodological commitment.
