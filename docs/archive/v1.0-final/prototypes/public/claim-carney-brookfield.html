CLAIM ID 1f2a3b4c FACTUAL LAYER VERSION 3 · CURRENT UPDATED MAR 14, 2026

Carney served on Brookfield Asset Management's board of directors as Vice Chair from August 2020 to December 2024.

GRADE A VERIFIED · GROUNDED LINCHPIN
DATA
Brookfield's 2020 annual report (filed SEDAR+ March 2021) lists Carney as Vice Chair, appointed August 2020. The 2024 annual report and SEDAR+ filing 2024-12-04 record his departure effective December 4, 2024. The dates are unambiguous; the corporate record is primary.
WARRANT
A Vice Chair role at a publicly-traded asset management firm involves: (a) fiduciary responsibility to the firm, (b) compensation in cash and equity that creates ongoing financial interest, (c) participation in strategic decisions about firm investments and policy positions, and (d) public association of the individual with the firm's interests. These four characteristics together constitute "service on the board" in the sense relevant to subsequent conflict-of-interest analysis.
BACKING
The warrant rests on (a) standard corporate-governance literature on board responsibilities, (b) Canadian conflict-of-interest jurisprudence holding that prior fiduciary roles create ongoing material interests, and (c) Brookfield's own SEDAR+ disclosures of Vice Chair compensation arrangements (cash component plus restricted share units with multi-year vesting).
QUALIFIER
Virtually certain (99–100%). The dates and role are documented in primary corporate filings; no plausible reading disputes them. The qualifier is at the maximum because the underlying data is verbatim corporate disclosure.
REBUTTAL
No serious rebuttal exists for the factual claim itself. Discussion exists about whether Vice Chair is a "fully active" or "advisory" role, but Brookfield's filings describe the role using standard active-board language and the compensation arrangements are consistent with active-board membership. Subsequent claims about conflicts of interest or implications for political appointments rest on this claim but are not the subject of this particular claim's rebuttal.
