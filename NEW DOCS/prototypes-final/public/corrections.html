CORRECTION · APR 18 CORRECTED 6 DAYS AFTER PUBLICATION
An article incorrectly stated that Brookfield's federal infrastructure contracts totaled $891 million.
What changed
The article said $891 million; the correct figure based on contracts.canada.ca filings is $847 million. The earlier figure double-counted one contract that had been amended rather than re-issued.
Why
The Verifier flagged the inconsistency on a re-run after a reader query. Original ingestion had treated an amendment record as a separate contract.
Article
The revolving door between Brookfield and Canadian public service.

CORRECTION · APR 09 CORRECTED 14 DAYS AFTER PUBLICATION
A claim's IPCC qualifier was downgraded from "very likely" to "likely" after additional evidence reduced confidence.
What changed
Claim "BoC will not lower rates before September 2026" had been published with very-likely qualifier. The April monetary policy report introduced language that meaningfully widened the range of plausible outcomes; the qualifier was lowered to likely.
Why
Calibration discipline: when in doubt between two adjacent qualifiers, the weaker one is correct. The new BoC language put the question in genuine doubt.
Affected
Monetary thesis tier projections regenerated
CORRECTION · MAR 21 CORRECTED 3 DAYS AFTER PUBLICATION
A source citation linked to the wrong section of a Hansard transcript.
What changed
The citation pointed to a different speaker's remarks than the one quoted. The substantive claim was correct; the link target was wrong.
Why
Manual citation entry error. The citation has been corrected; ingestion pipeline updated to validate Hansard speaker IDs against quoted text.
CORRECTION · MAR 04 CORRECTED 22 DAYS AFTER PUBLICATION
A predicted timeline was based on a misread of CMHC's release schedule.
What changed
The prediction "CMHC will publish revised mortgage-default modeling by Feb 28" was based on a misread of CMHC's announced release window. The window had been preliminary; CMHC published a revised schedule before the original publication of the prediction.
Why
The prediction has been retained as resolved-incorrect (rather than retracted) because it was a real call that turned out to be wrong; that is what the prediction ledger is for. Brier contribution stands.
Where
Prediction ledger entry
