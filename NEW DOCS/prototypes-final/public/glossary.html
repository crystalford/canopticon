Epistemic and methodological

Admiralty grading
A six-point scale (A–F) for source reliability, originating in NATO intelligence training. A is primary, official, verbatim. B is credible secondary. C is tertiary or partisan. D is anonymous or low-quality. E is known unreliable. F is cannot-assess. Every CANOPTICON source carries an Admiralty grade visible on every published claim.
Brier score
A measure of calibration for probabilistic predictions, equal to the mean squared error between predicted probability and actual outcome. Lower is better. Across many predictions, the rolling Brier score becomes the operator's track record. Named after Glenn Brier (1950). CANOPTICON publishes its rolling Brier on the prediction ledger.
Catuṣkoṭi
A four-cornered logic from early Buddhist philosophy (Nagarjuna, Madhyamaka school) admitting four truth states: affirmation, negation, both, neither. CANOPTICON uses it as a non-binary posture option for claims under contested framing.
Cynefin
A sense-making framework (David Snowden) sorting problems into clear, complicated, complex, chaotic, and disorder domains. Different problem types require different response patterns. Pronunciation: kuh-NEV-in.
Forensic intelligence
CANOPTICON's self-description. Not journalism, not academic analysis, not investigation in the police sense. Forensic: examining evidence methodically, reconstructing structure from documented facts, preserving chain of custody.
IPCC calibrated language
The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change's verbal probability scale, mapped to numeric ranges. Virtually certain (99–100%), very likely (90–100%), likely (66–100%), about as likely as not (33–66%), unlikely (0–33%), very unlikely (0–10%), exceptionally unlikely (0–1%). CANOPTICON uses these in tier projections so readers have a consistent verbal scale that maps to real probability ranges rather than vibes.
Lakatos — progressive vs degenerating
Imre Lakatos's argument that scientific theories should be evaluated by whether their broader research programme is progressive (predicts new phenomena that check out) or degenerating (only generates ad hoc adjustments). CANOPTICON applies this to its own theses.
Machloket
A term from rabbinic tradition for a disagreement that is preserved rather than resolved, because both sides contribute to the picture. CANOPTICON treats machloket as a legitimate contradiction posture.
McLuhan — the medium is the message
Marshall McLuhan's thesis that the form of a medium embeds itself in any message communicated through it. Foundational influence on CANOPTICON's framing layer and platform self-conception.
Toulmin warrant
In Stephen Toulmin's argument model, the warrant is the (often implicit) rule of inference that licenses moving from data to claim. CANOPTICON requires warrants to be explicit so they can be separately attacked. Most political disagreement is about warrants, not data.
Van Evera tests
Stephen Van Evera's four logical weights for qualitative evidence: straw-in-the-wind, hoop test, smoking gun, doubly decisive. Used to tag evidence weight rather than treating all evidence as equivalent.

CANOPTICON-specific

BLUF
"Bottom Line Up Front." Military and intelligence shorthand for leading with the conclusion. Every CANOPTICON thesis offers a BLUF — a one-sentence conclusion with IPCC likelihood qualifier.
Cascade
The system's primary workflow. Runs unattended from cron or on-demand. Processes pending sources through the Ingestion Critic, extracts claims, runs entity resolution, verifies new claims, generates Interrogator questions, runs the Gap Surfacer, calibrates resolved predictions, regenerates tier projections, audits assumptions weekly, detects convergences, and assembles the Live feed.
Coined phrase
Every landed thesis includes a coined phrase that compresses the structural finding into transmissible language. Not marketing — analytical work. Manufacturing consent. Surveillance capitalism. Shock doctrine.
Ingestion Critic
A role that runs on every incoming source before it writes to claim registries. Produces a written assessment: Admiralty grade, bias profile, contradiction check, paid-op flags. The operator approves, defers, or rejects.
Layered-topic template
Every contested topic CANOPTICON addresses decomposes into four analytical layers: Factual, Framing, Architectural, Distributive. Cross-layer contradictions are preserved, not flattened.
Linchpin
An assumption or claim whose failure would collapse an entire thesis, not merely weaken it. Flagged so the operator knows where the thesis is most vulnerable.
Operator
The person using CANOPTICON to do intelligence work. The operator is the gate at every paid operation, every publish transition, every contested merge. The system serves the operator; it does not autonomously act.
Production engine
The half of CANOPTICON that turns substrate into shippable content (videos, audio, articles, threads). Six states: Ready / Recording / Producing / Publish / Perf / Archive.
Root-striking discipline
Every investigation identifies its intervention level (symptom / mechanism / structural). Publishable theses operate at the structural level. From Thoreau: "There are a thousand hacking at the branches of evil to one who is striking at the root."
Substrate
The collective term for CANOPTICON's persistent data: claims, theses, articles, productions, entities, sources, assumptions, indicators, predictions, contradictions, questions, books, and their version histories. The system's memory and reasoning surface.
Undismissability architecture
Seven requirements every published thesis must satisfy: evidence density beyond rebuttal capacity, coined vocabulary, banked predictions (≥3), teachable diagram, methodology transparency, public graph artifact, named human accountability. Designed so critics must engage every piece of the evidence trail.

Operational

Cost gate
The single chokepoint through which every paid operation passes. Function checks ceiling, checks whitelist, either auto-approves, prompts the operator, or blocks. There is no fallback path that bypasses the gate. The discipline behind no-silent-spending.
Path A / Path B transcription
CANOPTICON's two-path discipline. Path A (free): YouTube captions via API. Path B (paid): AssemblyAI or transcription model when Path A returns nothing. LLM-as-transcriber explicitly forbidden because models hallucinate transcripts from URL metadata.

Canadian regional

BoC
Bank of Canada. Central bank. Responsible for monetary policy, currency issuance, and financial stability.
Big Five
Canada's five largest banks: RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC. Systemically important; regulated by OSFI.
Hansard
The verbatim transcript of debates in the Parliament of Canada. Published daily during sittings. A-grade source because it is verbatim and official.
OIC
Order-in-Council. A formal executive-branch instrument issued by the Governor-in-Council (Cabinet). Used for appointments and regulatory actions.
PBO
Parliamentary Budget Officer. An independent officer of Parliament who provides analysis of the federal budget. Appointed by the Governor-in-Council.
SEDAR+
The Canadian filing system for public-company disclosures. Primary source for Canadian corporate filings. A-grade.
